Flagship Project

Crop Coders
Kaggriculture

Kaggriculture is a simulation-based agricultural decision environment where an intelligent agent operates through a turn-based season. The Crop Coders architecture focuses on building a modular decision system capable of observing the environment, generating strategies, planning actions, learning from feedback, evaluating performance and improving its behaviour over repeated interactions.

The architecture is organized around specialized phases covering strategy generation, optimization and planning, observation and feedback, learning and adaptation, unit and integration testing, and system-level performance evaluation.
